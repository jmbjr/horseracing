How to play

  1. Every player antes one base bet and receives the configured number of cards. The balanced deck automatically scales with the players and hand size, leaving some cards hidden.
  2. Roll until the configured number of different horses are scratched. Matching cards are discarded. Scratch selection is free by default; an optional setup rule charges matching ticket holders.
  3. By default, rolling an already-scratched horse during scratching is a free reroll and the same player keeps the turn. The optional legacy rule makes that player pay and pass instead.
  4. During the race, each dice total moves that horse one space. Rolling a scratched horse always costs its displayed penalty.
  5. The first horse across the finish line wins. Each matching ticket receives one equal deck share of the pot; shares for hidden winning tickets carry into the next race.

Running out of chips: A player pays whatever they have left, is immediately eliminated at zero, receives no more turns, and cannot collect a winner payout.

Why are the lanes different lengths? Common totals are given longer tracks. Horse 7 needs 13 moves, while horses 2 and 12 need only 3, balancing their chances.
