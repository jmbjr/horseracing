How to play

  1. Every player antes one base bet. The 44 cards for horses 2–12 are dealt automatically.
  2. Roll until four different horses are scratched. Matching cards are discarded and cost 1×, 2×, 3×, then 4× the base bet per card.
  3. During the race, each dice total moves that horse one space. Rolling a scratched horse costs its scratch penalty.
  4. The first horse across the finish line wins. Each card matching it receives one quarter of the pot.

Why are the lanes different lengths? Common totals are given longer tracks. Horse 7 needs 13 moves, while horses 2 and 12 need only 3, balancing their chances.

Jack represents horse 11 and Queen represents horse 12. Game state is saved on this device.
